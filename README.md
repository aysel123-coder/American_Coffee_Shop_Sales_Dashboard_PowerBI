# ☕ American Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

The **American Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using **Microsoft Power BI**.

This dashboard provides a visual overview of coffee shop sales performance, customer transactions, product popularity, and revenue distribution across different cities and store locations.

The primary goal of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that support data-driven decision-making.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- 🧹 **Power Query** – Data preparation and transformation
- 🧮 **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPI analysis
- 📈 **Data Visualization** – Interactive charts, cards, and slicers
- 📋 **Business Intelligence** – Sales performance monitoring and reporting

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer important business questions:

1. 💰 What is the total sales revenue?
2. 🛒 How many products were sold?
3. 📍 Which cities generate the highest sales?
4. ☕ Which coffee products are the most popular?
5. 🕒 Which time of day generates the most revenue?
6. 📅 How does sales performance change monthly?
7. 🏪 Which store locations contribute the most revenue?
8. 🥤 How is revenue distributed between beverages and food?

## 📈 Dashboard Visualizations

### 🌎 1. Total Sales by City
**Visualization:** Horizontal Bar Chart

Compares sales performance across different cities, including Chicago, Seattle, and New York.

**Business Purpose:** Identify high-performing cities and potential opportunities for regional growth.

### 🕒 2. Sales by Daypart
**Visualization:** Column Chart

Analyzes revenue generated during:

- 🌅 Morning
- ☀️ Afternoon
- 🌙 Evening

**Business Insight:** Morning generates the highest revenue among the three periods shown, followed by afternoon and evening.

### 📅 3. Monthly Sales Trend
**Visualization:** Line Chart

Displays monthly revenue trends across January and February.

**Business Insight:** The displayed data shows a decrease in sales from January to February.

### ☕ 4. Total Quantity Sold by Product
**Visualization:** Column Chart

Compares product sales quantities across:

- Latte
- Iced Latte
- Mocha
- Tea
- Unknown

**Business Insight:** Latte has the highest quantity sold, followed by Iced Latte and Mocha.

### 🥤 5. Sales Distribution by Category
**Visualization:** Donut Chart

Illustrates the percentage of total revenue contributed by two categories.

| Category | Revenue | Revenue Share |
|---|---|---|
| 🥤 Beverage | $233.50 | 68.73% |
| 🍰 Food | $106.30 | 31.27% |

**Business Insight:** Beverages account for the majority of total sales revenue.

### 🏪 6. Sales by Store Location
**Visualization:** Horizontal Bar Chart

Compares revenue across three store locations:

- Downtown
- Market St
- Uptown

**Business Insight:** Downtown generates the highest revenue among the locations displayed.

## 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes interactive slicers that allow users to explore sales data dynamically.

### 📍 City Slicer
Allows users to filter sales performance by city, including Austin, Boston, Chicago, Dallas, Denver, New York, Portland, San Francisco, Seattle, and Unknown.

### 🥤 Category Slicer
Allows users to filter results by:

- Beverage
- Food

These interactive features help users analyze specific business segments without manually modifying the underlying data.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

Based on the dashboard visualizations:

- ☕ **Beverage Revenue Dominance:** Beverages contribute 68.73% of total revenue, making them the leading product category.
- 🌅 **Morning Sales Performance:** Morning generates the highest revenue among the displayed dayparts.
- 🏙️ **City Performance:** Chicago and Seattle show strong sales performance in the city comparison chart.
- 🏪 **Store Performance:** Downtown is the leading store location by revenue.
- 📉 **Monthly Revenue Trend:** Revenue declines between January and February in the displayed results.
- 🛍️ **Product Popularity:** Latte is the highest-selling product by quantity among the products shown.

*Note: These observations are based on the dashboard screenshot and may change when filters or the underlying dataset change.*

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

Based on the observed sales patterns, the following business strategies could be considered:

1. ☕ **Prioritize Popular Beverages:** Maintain sufficient inventory of high-demand coffee products such as Latte, Iced Latte, and Mocha.
2. 🌅 **Optimize Morning Operations:** Consider aligning staffing and inventory levels with stronger morning demand.
3. 📈 **Investigate Monthly Sales Changes:** Analyze the factors contributing to the January–February revenue decline.
4. 🏪 **Evaluate Store Performance:** Study successful practices at the Downtown location and assess whether they can be applied elsewhere.
5. 🍰 **Explore Food Sales Opportunities:** Consider promotions or product bundles to increase food category revenue.
